A few things
before you start.

Can I start with a free plan?

Yes. Free includes up to 3 campaigns and 300 live discount variants, plus automatic and code discounts.

Which plans include a free trial?

Essential and Pro each include a 3-day free trial.

How does yearly billing work?

With yearly billing, Essential is $83.90 per year, Pro is $191.90 per year, and Ultimate is $479.90 per year. Select Yearly above to compare annual prices.

Which plan supports more campaigns?

Free supports up to 3 campaigns, Essential up to 10, and Pro up to 30. Ultimate includes unlimited campaigns.

Where can I get help choosing a plan?

Visit our help center or contact support through the app. Pro includes priority support, and Ultimate includes VIP support.
