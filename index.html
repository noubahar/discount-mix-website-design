What types of discounts can I create?

Create product, volume, order and shipping discounts, plus Buy X Get Y offers, free gifts and more.

Can I combine different discounts?

Yes. Discount Mix supports combining product, order and shipping discounts, including multiple discount types in one code. Available combinations depend on your campaign configuration and Shopify's rules.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, a free plan is available. Visit the Shopify App Store listing for current plan limits and pricing.

Can I schedule my promotions?

Yes. You can schedule campaigns with start and end dates and use countdown timers to highlight your offers.

Where can I get help?

Contact the team through live chat in the app, or visit the help center.
